Chuck Norris facts

Chuck Norris facts are satirical factoids and hyperbolic claims that portray Chuck Norris as an all-powerful, larger-than-life character. These facts often highlight his supposed superhuman abilities, toughness, and invincibility. They have become a popular internet meme and are widely shared for humor and entertainment.

Chuck Norris facts have spread around, leading not only to translated versions in many languages, but also to the creation of a number of books. Allusions are also sometimes made to his use of roundhouse kicks, his beard, and his martial arts skills.
